Licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, provided in Fraunces-OFL.txt.

The font is instantiated at weight 800, optical size 72, SOFT 0, WONK 0.
Only lowercase x has been changed: the corresponding upright x is slanted
16 degrees and fitted to the original italic x ink width. Its advance and
the other glyphs are preserved. The derivative uses the family name
Kexin Serif to distinguish it from the original Fraunces family.
